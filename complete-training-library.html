Full contents

The seven courses included

Each course is included in full and unlocked by the same license code. Follow a link for the full contents of any course.

  1. Tactics Fundamentals

    Beginner. Forks, pins, skewers, discovered attacks and double attacks: short lessons on each idea, then training positions you solve on the board.

  2. Checkmate Patterns

    Beginner. The common mating patterns, how to recognize them, and training positions where you deliver the mate on the board.

  3. Opening Repertoire for Black

    Intermediate. A complete repertoire for Black against White’s main first moves, explained by plans, with a line explorer and a drill mode.

  4. Opening Repertoire for White

    Intermediate. A complete 1.e4 repertoire for White with the main plans and pawn structures, in a line explorer with a drill mode.

  5. Endgame Essentials

    Beginner to intermediate. King and pawn endings, basic rook endings and key theoretical positions, with practice where you convert or hold against exact replies.

  6. Positional Play & Strategy

    Intermediate to advanced. Pawn structures, outposts, good and bad pieces and quiet moves, with exercises on the board.

  7. Calculation & Visualization Training

    Intermediate to advanced. Forcing lines to calculate on the board, and visualization exercises where you follow moves without the board moving.

Using the library

A suggested order

The courses can be taken in any order, but this sequence moves from the foundations to the most demanding material.

  1. 01

    Start with tactics and mates

    Tactics Fundamentals and Checkmate Patterns build the pattern recognition that every other course relies on.

  2. 02

    Add endgames early

    Endgame Essentials sits comfortably alongside the beginner courses and runs into intermediate material.

  3. 03

    Choose your openings

    The repertoires for White and Black give you a complete, connected set of lines once the basics are in place.

  4. 04

    Move on to strategy and calculation

    Positional Play & Strategy and Calculation & Visualization Training are the most demanding courses and work best last.
