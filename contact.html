Contact

Contact Fiancho Chess

Questions about a course, a license, an order, a refund or a correction? Fiancho Chess handles support by email, and a person reads and answers every message.

Support email

Response time

We reply to every message within two business days.

What to include

To help us answer in one reply, please include:

  • Your order number
  • The email address you used at checkout
  • The name of the course
  • For a problem with a position, the Report a problem link in the trainer fills in the details for you

Common requests

  • A lost license code

    If the course is still unlocked in your browser, the code is on the course’s product page. Otherwise, write to us with your order number and we’ll send a replacement code.

  • Using a course on another device

    Enter the same license code in the Training Room or on the product page on that device. To move your progress, export a backup and import it there.

  • Refunds

    Any purchase can be refunded in full within 14 days, no reason required. Include your order number. See the Refund Policy.

  • A problem with a position

    Use the Report a problem link under the position. Confirmed corrections are applied to the course automatically.

  • Licenses for coaches and clubs

    The standard license is for personal use. Ask us about a separate license for using the courses with students or club members.

  • Privacy requests

    To access, correct or delete the order information we hold, or to object to its use, see the Privacy Policy and write to us.
