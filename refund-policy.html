The 14-day refund

You can request a full refund on any Fiancho Chess purchase within 14 days of buying it, whether or not you have started training. You don’t need to give a reason. This applies to every product, including the Complete Training Library, which is refunded as a single purchase.

How to request a refund

Send your request through the Contact page and include your order number. Including the email address you used at checkout helps us find your order quickly.

How refunds are paid

Refunds go back to your original payment method. They are issued within 5 business days of your request. Your payment provider’s processing time may add a few days before the money appears in your account.

Your license after a refund

The license ends when a purchase is refunded, and the license code may no longer be used.

Your statutory rights

This policy is in addition to any rights you have under consumer protection law. See Your consumer rights.

Contact

Questions about refunds can be sent through the Contact page. We reply within two business days.
