Full contents

What the course contains

The course is organized into the sections below, exactly as they appear in the trainer.

  1. Endgame principles

    King activity, passed pawns, and the king on the sixth rank in front of its pawn.

    lesson · worked example

  2. King and pawn endings

    The square of the pawn and the opposition, with practice positions to win and to hold.

    lesson · worked example · 4 training positions · 4 practice positions against exact replies

  3. Basic rook endings

    Active rooks, cutting off the king, and rooks behind passed pawns.

    lesson · 4 training positions

  4. Key theoretical positions

    Building the bridge to win and the third-rank defense to draw, stepped through move by move.

    lesson · 2 worked examples

  5. Converting an advantage

    Checkmate with king and queen and with king and rook, and promoting a pawn, against exact defense.

    lesson · 3 practice positions against exact replies

  6. Training positions

    King and pawn and rook endings where you find the winning or saving move.

    lesson · 6 training positions

Who it’s for

  • Players who reach endgames but are unsure of the standard techniques.
  • Players who want to learn the key theoretical positions properly, one at a time.
  • Anyone who wants to practise basic endings against a defender that never makes a mistake.

Who it isn’t for

  • Players looking for a deep reference on rare or complex endings.
  • Complete newcomers still learning how the pieces move.
  • Anyone looking for video lessons, live classes or personal coaching.
