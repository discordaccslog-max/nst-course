About

About Fiancho Chess

Fiancho Chess makes interactive chess training programs that run in your web browser: short lessons and positions you play on the board, at your own pace.

What we make

Each Fiancho Chess course covers one part of the game: tactics, checkmate patterns, an opening repertoire for White or for Black, endgames, positional play, or calculation and visualization. The Complete Training Library brings all seven together under one license.

Every course follows the same rhythm. An idea is explained in plain language and shown in a worked example on the board, then you practise it in positions where you make the moves yourself. The trainer checks every move, offers hints and the full solution, and brings back the positions you missed.

The opening courses work differently: a line explorer explains the plan behind every move, and a drill mode lets you play your side of each line until you know it. In the endgame course, practice positions are played against exact replies from complete endgame tables.

Why interactive training

Reading about chess helps, but finding the move yourself is what builds the habit.

  • You set the pace

    There is no schedule and nothing to keep up with. Spend five minutes on one position or an evening on a whole section.

  • Thinking comes first

    Positions are set before their solutions, so you do the work of finding the move. Hints and solutions are there when you need them, not before.

  • It remembers what you missed

    Positions you get wrong go into a review queue, so you come back to exactly the ideas that need more work.

  • It stays private

    Your licenses and progress are stored only in your own browser. Nothing you do in the trainer is sent anywhere.

How the material is put together

All explanations and annotations are original. Training positions are either composed for these courses or drawn from a public-domain (CC0) position database.

Every solution is checked with a chess engine before release, and any move that is equally good is accepted, so a correct alternative is never marked wrong. No engine runs in the product itself.

Chess analysis can still contain mistakes, so every position has a Report a problem link. Confirmed corrections are applied to the course automatically.

What Fiancho Chess doesn’t do

Fiancho Chess sells self-study training programs and nothing else. There is no video, no live instruction, no coaching, and no feedback on your own games. We don’t promise any particular result: how much a course helps depends on how you study and play, where you start from, and many other individual factors.

Fiancho Chess is independent. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by any chess federation, rating organization, chess website or software maker.
