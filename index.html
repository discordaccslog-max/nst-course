In every course

What’s included in every course

Each course is delivered as a single .zip file. Inside are two things that work together: a workbook to read and PGN files to play through.

  • PDF workbook

    Written explanations of each idea, board diagrams wherever a position needs to be seen, and training positions grouped by theme. Read it on screen or print it for your own study.

  • PGN files

    Every example, training position and solution line in standard PGN format, ready to load into any chess software or online analysis board that opens PGN files.

  • Worked solutions

    Each training position has a fully worked solution: the main line, the important defensive tries, and a plain explanation of why the moves work.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

What do I receive after I pay, and how?

Right after your payment is confirmed, the payment provider’s order confirmation screen shows a download link for your course, and the same link is emailed to the address you used at checkout. The link downloads a single .zip file containing the course’s PDF workbook and PGN files (for the Complete Training Library, all seven courses, each in its own folder).

There is no license key, no account and no physical shipment. If the email doesn’t arrive within an hour, check your spam folder, then contact us through the Contact page and we’ll resend the link. The full steps are in the Delivery Policy.

What exactly do I receive?

A single .zip file containing a PDF workbook and PGN files. The PDF holds the explanations, board diagrams, training positions and fully worked solutions. The PGN files hold every position and line from the course, so you can play through them in any chess software or online analysis board that opens PGN files. The Complete Training Library contains all seven courses, each in its own folder.

Is there any video, live instruction or coaching?

No. Fiancho Chess products are written self-study materials: a PDF workbook and PGN files. There is no video, no live instruction, no coaching, and no feedback on your games. You work through the material on your own, at your own pace.

My download hasn’t arrived. What should I do?

The download link is sent by email immediately after payment and is also shown on the order confirmation screen. If the email hasn’t arrived within an hour, check your spam or junk folder. If it isn’t there, contact us through the Contact page with your order number and the email address you used at checkout, and we’ll resend the link.
