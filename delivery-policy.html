Digital products only

All Fiancho Chess products are digital. Each one is a training program that runs in your web browser and is unlocked with a license code. There is no physical shipment.

How delivery works, step by step

  1. Agree and pay. On the product page, tick the box to agree to the Terms of Service and Refund Policy, then pay on the payment provider’s checkout page.
  2. Get your license immediately. As soon as your payment is confirmed, you are shown the order confirmation page. It issues your license code and activates it in that browser. You can copy the code, save the confirmation as a PDF, or print it.
  3. Start training. The Start training button on the confirmation page takes you straight into your course.
  4. Use another device. To use the course on another device, enter the same license code in the Training Room or on the course’s product page there.

When you receive it

Delivery is immediate: your license code is issued on the confirmation page the moment your payment is confirmed. There is no waiting period and no manual processing.

Lost licenses

If the course is still unlocked in your browser, your code is shown on the course’s product page and in any backup file you exported. If you have lost it, contact us through the Contact page with your order number, and we will send you a replacement code.

Corrections and updates

Corrected versions of a course are applied automatically: the trainer picks up the new version the next time you open it while online, and your progress is kept.

How long access lasts

Online access to your courses continues for as long as Fiancho Chess operates. Once loaded, the trainer also works offline on your device.

What you need

A current version of Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari on a computer, tablet or phone. Licenses and progress are stored in your browser, so use the Export backup feature in the Training Room to keep a copy.
