Full contents

What the course contains

The course is organized into the sections below, exactly as they appear in the trainer.

  1. What makes a position tactical

    The warning signs that a tactic is available: loose pieces, pieces lined up on one line, and an exposed king.

    lesson · worked example

  2. Forks

    One piece attacking two targets, with the knight fork at the centre.

    lesson · worked example · 4 training positions

  3. Pins

    Absolute and relative pins, and how to win material against a pinned piece.

    lesson · worked example · 4 training positions

  4. Skewers

    Attacking a valuable piece so that the piece behind it falls.

    lesson · worked example · 4 training positions

  5. Discovered attacks

    Unmasking an attack by moving another piece, including discovered check and double check.

    lesson · worked example · 4 training positions

  6. Double attacks

    Moves that create two threats at once, especially with the queen.

    lesson · worked example · 4 training positions

  7. Mixed training positions

    Positions where the theme isn’t announced, so you have to recognize it first.

    lesson · 7 training positions

Who it’s for

  • Players who know the rules and how the pieces move, and want a systematic introduction to tactics.
  • Players who often miss short tactics in their own games and want to practise the patterns one at a time.
  • Learners who like to solve positions on a board and get instant feedback on every move.

Who it isn’t for

  • Complete newcomers still learning how the pieces move. The course assumes you know the rules.
  • Experienced players looking for long, complex combinations.
  • Anyone looking for video lessons, live classes or personal coaching.
