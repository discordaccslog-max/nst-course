Full contents

The seven courses included

Each course is included in full, with its own PDF workbook and PGN files. Follow a link for the full contents of any course.

  1. Tactics Fundamentals

    Beginner. Forks, pins, skewers, discovered attacks and double attacks, each explained with examples and followed by training positions with worked solutions.

  2. Checkmate Patterns

    Beginner. The common mating patterns, how to recognize them, and training positions for spotting them in play.

  3. Opening Repertoire for Black

    Intermediate. A complete repertoire for Black against White’s main first moves, organized by the ideas and plans behind each line rather than memorized move orders.

  4. Opening Repertoire for White

    Intermediate. A complete repertoire for White built around 1.e4, with the main plans, typical pawn structures, and how to handle Black’s common replies.

  5. Endgame Essentials

    Beginner to intermediate. King and pawn endings, basic rook endings, and the key theoretical positions every player should know, with training positions to practice converting them.

  6. Positional Play & Strategy

    Intermediate to advanced. Pawn structures, piece activity, weak squares and planning in quiet positions, explained through annotated examples and training exercises.

  7. Calculation & Visualization Training

    Intermediate to advanced. Structured exercises for calculating forcing lines and visualizing positions without moving pieces, increasing in difficulty across the course.

Using the library

A suggested study order

The courses can be studied in any order, but this sequence moves from the foundations to the most demanding material.

  1. 01

    Start with tactics and mates

    Tactics Fundamentals and Checkmate Patterns build the pattern recognition that every other course relies on.

  2. 02

    Add endgames early

    Endgame Essentials sits comfortably alongside the beginner courses and runs into intermediate material.

  3. 03

    Choose your openings

    The repertoires for White and Black give you a complete, connected set of lines once the basics are in place.

  4. 04

    Move on to strategy and calculation

    Positional Play & Strategy and Calculation & Visualization Training are the most demanding courses and work best last.
